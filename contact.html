Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 407 Treasury Clearance?

Section 407 specifies one-time identity and contribution audit requirements ensuring 100% US Dollar stablecoin backing and legally protected yield allocations for supporters.

How are weekly yield profits calculated?

Yield distributions accrue automatically on active account balances at a 32.00% weekly equivalent yield rate based on institutional short-term Treasury asset yields.
